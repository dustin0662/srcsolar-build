FAQ

Before you air down

Do I have to own a Ford Raptor?

Raptors lead the pack, but we welcome any capable off-road truck. If your rig can hold the line, you're good to roll on a convoy. Members-only runs are built around the Raptor community.

I'm new to off-roading — can I still come?

Absolutely. All experience levels are welcome. Most runs mix technical sections with wide-open stretches, and there are always spotters and support if you need them. Start with a convoy.

What's the difference between a convoy and a members-only run?

Convoys are open to anyone who wants to run with the group and see what NMRR is like. Members-only runs are exclusive to members — more of them, on trails we keep close to the crew.

How much is membership and what do I get?

$300 a year. That covers 10–12 members-only runs at no extra charge, exclusive trail access, early registration, a goodie bag (shirt, hat, stickers), and discounts with our partner shops.

What should I bring on a run?

Full tank, recovery points, a way to air down and back up, water, and basic recovery gear if you have it. The crew carries backup — but come prepared and ready for a long day in the desert.
